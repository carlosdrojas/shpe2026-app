# SHPE 2026 Prep — offline app

Single-page, offline-capable prep app for the SHPE 2026 National Convention. Lists exhibitor and interview booths, and lets you mark companies and keep notes.

- Open the site once on Wi-Fi, then add it to your home screen. It keeps working without a connection.
- Notes and statuses are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Backup → Download to export.
- Roster data comes from the public EXPOCAD floor plans for the event (Career Fair and Interview Booths).
